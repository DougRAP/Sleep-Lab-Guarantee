The Guarantee

If after 30 days You are not sleeping soundly on Your new Mattress Set, subject to the terms of this Guarantee, You may make a one-time exchange of Your Mattress Set for a new mattress (and if applicable base) of equal or greater value that is a better fit for You.

  • Exchanges under this Guarantee are not available until the 31st day after the date You first take delivery of Your Mattress Set and must occur on or before the 90th day after that delivery date.
  • All exchanges must be made with and in a location operated by the Dealer that sold You the Mattress Set.
  • This Guarantee provides a one-time exchange credit only; no refunds are available under this Guarantee even if You are unable to find a new mattress that You like.
  • Where applicable, both sleep partners must be present in the Dealer's store to select a new Mattress Set to ensure the right fit.
  • You may make ONLY ONE exchange under this Guarantee and will not be able to later exchange the replacement mattress set.
  • You must follow the procedures of this Guarantee to make an exchange and present to the Dealer a return authorization issued by Us (an "RA") to initiate an exchange.
  • To be eligible for exchange, Your Mattress Set must be in like-new condition, free of stains, odors, hair and damage. The law and model label tags must be attached and unaltered. Please note that removing the law or label tags voids this 90-Night Comfort Guarantee. We strongly recommend that You use a waterproof mattress protector to maintain Your eligibility to exchange Your Mattress Set under this 90-Night Comfort Guarantee.
  • You must pay to the Dealer a $199 comfort exchange fee when making an exchange under this Guarantee. This fee includes shipping and delivery fees for delivery and pickup within 50 miles from a location operated by the Dealer; shipping and delivery greater than 50 miles will require additional fees. In addition, You must pay to the Dealer any difference between the exchange credit authorized by RAP and the Dealer's selling price for the replacement mattress and/or matching base.
  • A restocking fee applies to California King size Mattress Sets and must be paid to the Dealer before the exchange.
  • A mattress or base received as a replacement under any manufacturer warranty, retailer warranty, service contract, protection plan, insurance policy, or other benefit program is not eligible for exchange under this Guarantee.
  • This Guarantee excludes adjustable bed bases, which are non-returnable and non-refundable.
