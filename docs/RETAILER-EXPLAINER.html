How the 90-Night Comfort Guarantee App Works

A guide for the City Mattress team · Program administered by Risk Assurance Partners, LLC (RAP)

The Program in One Paragraph

Every qualifying mattress purchase includes RAP's Limited 90-Night Comfort Guarantee: if the customer isn't sleeping soundly after a 30-night adjustment period, they may make a one-time exchange between night 31 and night 90 for a mattress of equal or greater value. RAP is the guarantor and administrator — RAP reviews every request, decides eligibility, and issues the exchange authorization. City Mattress performs the exchange in-store, schedules the pickup, and collects the $199 comfort exchange fee (plus any Cal King restocking fee and any price difference). Your team never has to adjudicate a comfort claim — RAP does that.

What the Customer Sees

  1. They visit the Sleep Lab site. No account, no login, no receipt hunting required to get started. The front page welcomes them, links to the full guarantee terms, and asks for a few basics: their name, their sales order number or the ZIP code where the mattress was delivered, and an email or mobile number.
  2. The app walks them through a short guided request. Purchase details (model, purchase and delivery dates) — the app calculates their night count automatically and tells them where they stand. If it's too early (before night 31), they can choose to have the request start automatically on night 31, or ask for a call from a RAP agent. Nobody gets turned away.
  3. They confirm the guarantee's conditions. A simple checklist mirrors the guarantee terms: clean and sanitary, law and model tags attached, like-new condition, original owner, household use, and so on.
  4. Photos are optional. The app asks for three quick shots (law tag, model tag, top-down) plus the receipt, with instructions to remove bedding first — and explains that photos speed the review. Since RAP can send a technician, skipping photos never blocks the request.
  5. They get a claim number. On submit, the customer receives a claim number in the form CG######. That number is their single reference — it's what they'll quote on the phone, use to track progress online, and what your team will see on the dashboard.
If a customer calls your store about comfort (not damage or defects): point them to the app, or to RAP directly at 1-855-513-5435 / claims@raptns.com. Damage, defects, and warranty issues stay with the manufacturer process as they do today — the Comfort Guarantee doesn't cover them.

What Happens Behind the Scenes

  1. RAP reviews the request. RAP's service team pulls each new claim into its production system and verifies eligibility against the guarantee terms.
  2. RAP may inspect. If an inspection is needed, RAP arranges a technician visit — and always calls the customer before anyone comes out.
  3. RAP issues the exchange authorization. Approved exchanges get an authorization sent to the customer by text or email, and posted to the dealer dashboard so your team sees the same document.
  4. City Mattress completes the exchange. Your team schedules the pickup and the in-store reselection (both sleep partners present where applicable), collects the $199 comfort exchange fee, any Cal King restocking fee, and any price difference between the exchange credit and the replacement's selling price.

Why This Is Good for the Store

  • Your floor team is out of the claims business. Comfort complaints route to RAP's process and RAP's phone line, not your sales counter.
  • Every exchange is authorized before it happens. No exchange occurs without RAP's authorization in hand, and the terms (fees, one-time limit, like-new condition, tag checks) are enforced consistently by the administrator.
  • The 30-night adjustment period is built in. The app doesn't offer an exchange before night 31 — customers are guided to settle in first, which resolves many comfort concerns on its own.
  • You keep the customer. An exchange keeps the sale in your store — the replacement is selected in your showroom, of equal or greater value.
